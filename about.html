Background

Built on university research, tested on a global stage

D.Company Ltd. develops L:BRN with the Ewha Brain Institute, grounding its cognitive measures in published neuroscience. The company validated its MVP with 150 university students, then exhibited at SWITCH Singapore 2025 and pitched to global investors.

Research partner

Ewha Brain Institute

Korea's leading neuroscience and cognitive research institute, and our core scientific partner under an official MOU. Its director co-founded D.Company, and our patents and features are developed with his research team.

Backed by

Ewha Womans University Technology Holdings

University technology holdings backing, giving D.Company Ltd. direct access to academic research pipelines and technology transfer.

Overall roadmap

From a launched app to a full learning ecosystem

Each phase adds a layer to the same data foundation. The app came first because it is what generates the behavioural signal the device and the center both depend on.

Phase 1 · Done Through Jan 2026

AI app developed and launched

The AI learning app was built and shipped, putting goal management, study teams, brain games, and learning reports in front of learners.

Phase 2 · In progress 2026 – 2027

AI features mature, institutional SaaS, neuromodulation device development

App intelligence deepens, an analytics SaaS product opens the platform to institutions, and development of the neuromodulation device begins in earnest.

Phase 3 2028 onward

Neuromodulation device and offline center launch, global expansion

The neuromodulation device and the offline learning center come to market together, completing the ecosystem, followed by expansion beyond Korea.
